Why Deith Zireael

Both words are Elder Speech, the elvish tongue in the Witcher. Deith means flame. Zireael means swallow, which is what Ciri gets called. I picked Deith Zireael as my gaming ID back when I first played the Witcher 3, and have kept it ever since.

Why I make games

The Witcher 3 was my first RPG and is still one of my favorites. It's a big reason I got into making games.

The other big influence is GRIS. It got me into indie games, and opened my eyes to how varied they are. Indie developers make all kinds of games: some are works of art, some are heartwarming, some are like nothing you've played before.
